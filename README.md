📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer sales data using an interactive Tableau dashboard to uncover patterns, trends, and insights that can help guide business decisions.

🔍 Data Insights
The dashboard provides multiple layers of analysis, including:
✅ Revenue per Month: Fluctuations in monthly sales from October 2020 to September 2021, with a peak in November 2020 at $57.7M.
✅ Revenue per State: Geographic revenue distribution across U.S. states, highlighting top-performing regions like California and Texas.
✅ Revenue per Age Group: Customer age groups 30–60 generate the highest revenues (~$39–$41M), while under-20 and over-70 groups contribute less.
✅ Correlation Between Quantity & Discount: A scatterplot reveals how discount percentages relate to quantities ordered, showing clusters and potential discount strategies.
✅ Revenue Percentage per Region: The South leads with 38.37% of total revenue, followed by the Midwest and West.
✅ Revenue per Category per Gender: Female and male customers both generate the highest sales in Appliances and Fashion categories, with similar patterns across other segments.

⚙️ Tools Used
Tableau for data visualization and dashboard design

Data source: Customer sales dataset (likely Excel or CSV format)

Analytical focus: Time trends, geographic insights, customer segmentation, product category breakdowns

💡 Key Findings
Holiday months (like November) show sales spikes, suggesting strong seasonal patterns.

Southern and Midwestern regions are key drivers of overall revenue.

Discounting strategies have mixed impacts on order quantity, requiring deeper analysis.

Both genders show similar buying behavior in top categories, which could inform marketing strategies.
